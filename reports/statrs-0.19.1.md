The input p is an exact double and the quantities a quantile method needs, p - 1/2 (exact for 1/4 <= p <= 1 by Sterbenz's lemma), 1 - p (exact for p >= 1/2) and log p, are exact or correctly rounded, so nothing beyond a few ulps of arithmetic is unavoidable (Wichura's AS 241 reaches about 1e-16). The quantile is ill-conditioned in p near p = 1/2 (|p / (x phi(x))| grows without bound) but that only matters for an inexact p. 1e-13 as for the other functions; SciPy's worst on this grid is 2.1e-16
- **Worst case:** p = 9.53125e-1 gives 1.6759397227734443e0 (mpmath 1.6759397227734438e0), rel. error 2.6e-16
