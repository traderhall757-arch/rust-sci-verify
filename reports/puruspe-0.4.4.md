## `puruspe::Kn`

- **Dataset:** `reference/data/kv.jsonl`: Modified Bessel function of the second kind K_nu(x), integer order
- **References:** mpmath 1.4.1, SciPy 1.18.1
- **Grid:** nu in {0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100} x x in {0.01, 0.0631, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 700}; K_100(0.01) overflows
